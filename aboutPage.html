About EnableWorks

Our platform was created to help persons with disabilities (PWDs) access fair and inclusive employment opportunities. Despite advancements in workplace inclusivity, many PWDs still face barriers due to limited accessible job options and lack of transparent information.

We focus on supporting job seekers with physical, sensory, or mental health disabilities who often struggle to find workplaces that understand and accommodate their needs. Employment is essential for dignity, independence, and quality of life, yet unemployment remains high within the PWD community.

Through the EnableWorks Board, we provide an accessible and easy-to-use platform where PWDs can find jobs tailored to their requirements. Employers can view accessibility needs through clear profiles, allowing for better job matching and inclusive hiring.

Guided by SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities, our mission is to reduce employment gaps and ensure that disability is never a barrier to meaningful work.
