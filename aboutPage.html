About Us

Building accessible opportunities for PWD job seekers

About EnableWorks

EnableWorks is a web-based platform created to help Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) find fair, accessible, and inclusive employment opportunities. It serves as a job suggestion and referral hub that connects PWD job seekers to verified job postings from inclusive employers.

Many PWD job seekers still experience difficulty finding workplaces that clearly provide accessible job options and understand different accessibility needs. EnableWorks helps reduce this problem by organizing PWD-friendly opportunities in one simple and easy-to-use website.

Instead of making users search through many job platforms manually, EnableWorks provides job cards, filtering options, accessibility matching, and direct application links to official job postings. This makes the job search process easier, faster, and more focused on the needs of PWD job seekers.

Mission and Purpose

Using web technology to create a meaningful and inclusive solution

Our Mission

Our mission is to make job searching more accessible for Persons with Disabilities by providing a simple platform that matches users with job opportunities based on their skills, work preferences, and accessibility needs.

Who We Support

EnableWorks supports job seekers with physical, sensory, and other accessibility needs who may struggle to find clear information about inclusive workplaces and suitable job roles.

How The Website Helps

The website helps by centralizing PWD-friendly job opportunities, showing important job details, and giving users direct links to official application pages. This creates a smoother bridge between qualified PWD applicants and inclusive employers.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

EnableWorks is aligned with Sustainable Development Goal 10 because it aims to reduce barriers in employment access and support equal opportunities for Persons with Disabilities.

SDG 10 Alignment

EnableWorks supports SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities by helping lessen the gap between PWD job seekers and accessible employment opportunities. The platform focuses on inclusion by making job information easier to find, understand, and access.

Through features such as accessibility matching, skill-based job suggestions, preferred work setup filtering, job details pop-ups, and direct apply buttons, the website helps users find opportunities that fit their needs. This supports dignity, independence, and better quality of life for PWD job seekers.

As a DCS / Web Systems project, EnableWorks shows how a website can become more than a digital output. It becomes a practical tool that responds to a real community issue and promotes inclusive technology for social impact.
